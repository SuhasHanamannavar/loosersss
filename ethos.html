Our Ethos

Where the wild leads.

We do not design the forest. We read it, then plant the layers that hold the place together, and step back.

Water lines, shade, stone, and soil tell us what a site already wants to be. Restoration here means thin intervention: native ground cover first, ferns and shrubs next, canopy natives last, then long seasons of quiet observation.

Let the ground teach.

I

Read the land

Moss beds, exposed roots, and patches of bare soil mark where water sits and where shade is missing.

II

Plant in layers

Ground cover, ferns, shrubs, and canopy natives go in together so moisture and shade hold themselves up.

III

Return each season

Growth is recorded, losses are replaced, and the woody plants are allowed to take over on their own schedule.
